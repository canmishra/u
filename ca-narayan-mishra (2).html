Common Challenges I Solve for Clients

If any of these feel like your situation, we need to talk.

😟
"My tax bills are too high and I don't know why."
Most businesses overpay tax simply because they lack a proactive advisor. I conduct a full tax health check and build a legal, structured plan to bring your tax liability down — not at filing time, but throughout the year.
😰
"Audit season is always a nightmare for us."
Audits feel chaotic when books aren't maintained properly year-round. With my ongoing bookkeeping oversight and audit-ready protocols, my clients sail through audit season without panic or last-minute scrambling.
😤
"We got a GST notice and have no idea what to do."
GST notices are more common than ever. I handle the full representation — from understanding the notice to drafting replies and appearing before authorities — protecting your business at every step.
🤔
"I don't trust my current financial numbers."
Bad data leads to bad decisions. I implement structured MIS and reporting frameworks so your financials become a reliable compass for growth — not a quarterly guessing game.

About the Firm

G R Kumar & Co LLP

Founded in 1985 by the late Mr. Goda Ravi Kumar — who left a senior role at PricewaterhouseCoopers to build something of his own — G R Kumar & Co LLP has grown over four decades into one of India's most respected independent CA firms.

The firm evolved through merger of several established practices, some operational since 1977, bringing together a multi-disciplinary team of rare depth. Today the firm serves Indian corporates, PSUs, banks, and global MNCs from nine offices across the country.

Our philosophy has never changed: "Trusted Business Advisors" — blending rigorous expertise with genuinely personalised service.

Category I CA Firm — Empanelled with ICAI, CAG & RBI for audit of PSUs and Banks.
